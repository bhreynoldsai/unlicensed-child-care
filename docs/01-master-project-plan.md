**TRUE NORTH STRATEGIES**

**Master Project Plan**

*Grassroots activation platform for the licensed child care community*

Georgia Child Care Grassroots Activation Initiative \| August 2026 \|
Internal Working Document — Draft for Discussion

1\. Purpose

Build and launch a grassroots activation platform that organizes the
licensed child care community in Georgia — owners, regional managers,
corporate staff, directors, and teachers — into an identifiable,
contactable, and mobilizable advocacy network. The near-term policy
objective is to counter the competitive and regulatory imbalance created
by license-exempt child care programs by equipping the licensed
community to contact their state legislators quickly and credibly when
legislation or rulemaking is in play.

2\. Background and Problem Statement

Georgia law and DECAL (Bright from the Start) rules exempt a defined set
of programs from child care licensure — including certain short-duration
programs, school-age recreational programs, faith-based and
membership-organization programs, and others enumerated in DECAL's
exemption rules. Licensed providers absorb the full cost of licensure
compliance (staffing ratios, training, facility standards, inspections)
while exempt programs serving similar families operate without those
requirements. This creates both a health-and-safety gap for children and
an uneven playing field for licensed operators.

The licensed community is large but unorganized as a political
constituency. Individual centers rarely know who their legislators are,
and no shared infrastructure exists to move them to act on a deadline.
This initiative closes that gap.

3\. Objectives

- Recruit a verified supporter base from licensed child care
  organizations, segmented by role (owner, regional manager, corporate
  staff, director, teacher, other).

- Match every supporter to their Georgia House and Senate districts (and
  congressional district) from their home address, so activation targets
  the legislators who actually represent them.

- Enable rapid, role-appropriate activation: pre-drafted emails, call
  scripts, and testimony requests deliverable within hours of a
  committee notice.

- Demonstrate constituent density per legislative district — counts of
  owners, staff, and classrooms represented — as a lobbying asset.

6\. Roles and Governance

- **Sponsor:** The client organization (to be confirmed) owns the
  supporter data and the policy agenda.

- **True North Strategies:** Program design, platform delivery, campaign
  management, legislative intelligence, and reporting.

- **Center leadership (owners/directors):** Primary recruitment channel;
  distribute sign-up link and QR materials to staff on a strictly
  voluntary basis.

- **Counsel (to be engaged):** Review of consent language,
  lobbying-disclosure posture, and employer-solicitation guardrails
  before launch.

8\. Risks and Mitigations

| **Risk**                                                                       | **Mitigation**                                                                                                                       |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Employer pressure perception — staff feel compelled to enroll or act           | Voluntary-participation language everywhere; no enrollment reporting to supervisors at individual level; counsel review (see Doc 03) |
| TCPA exposure from texting supporters                                          | Express written consent at sign-up, separate SMS checkbox, opt-out honored automatically (Doc 03)                                    |
| Data breach of home addresses and phones                                       | Minimum-necessary collection, encrypted storage, access controls, retention limits (Doc 03)                                          |
| Low activation despite enrollment                                              | Role-based asks sized to effort; owners asked for testimony, teachers for one-click emails (Doc 04)                                  |
| Countermobilization by exempt-program constituencies (faith-based, recreation) | Message discipline: child safety and level playing field, not attacks on churches or rec programs (Doc 04)                           |
